Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers.

What is StaffBot.my? ▼
StaffBot.my is a Digital Employee as a Service platform. We provide dedicated AI agents that work for your business — handling WhatsApp inquiries, executing tasks, and integrating with your tools.
How is this different from ChatGPT? ▼
Unlike ChatGPT which is a general chatbot, StaffBot.my agents are dedicated to YOUR business. They learn your SOP, remember your customers, access your data, and execute tasks autonomously — like a real employee.
Can I try before buying? ▼
Yes! Every plan comes with a 7-day free trial. No credit card required. You get full access to all features during the trial period.
Is my data secure? ▼
Absolutely. Each client gets an isolated Docker container with their own environment. Your data is encrypted and never shared. We follow strict data isolation practices.
Can I cancel anytime? ▼
Yes. No long-term contracts. Cancel anytime from your dashboard. If you cancel mid-month, you'll still have access until the end of the billing period.
